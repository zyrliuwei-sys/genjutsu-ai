**The short answer:** the Genjutsu trend works best when the swap _is_ the joke — someone ordinary appears somewhere they could never be. Pick a scene people already recognise, swap in a person your audience knows, and put the twist in the first second of the caption. Below are 12 ideas that follow that formula, grouped by who you are making the video for.

## The formula behind every Genjutsu trend post

1. **A scene with a hook** — the moment people would rewatch even without the swap.
2. **A person your audience knows** — you, your friend, your boss, your mum. A stranger swapped in is just an AI demo.
3. **A caption that sets up the twist** — tell viewers what they are about to see _before_ the reveal, not after.

If one of the three is missing, the clip usually reads as "cool AI effect" and gets scrolled past.

## Ideas for friends and group chats

1. **"POV: your friend finally made it."** Put the friend who never leaves the house into a runway walk or a fashion editorial. The contrast is the punchline.
2. **The birthday cameo.** Swap the birthday person into the most dramatic scene you can find and post it at midnight instead of a card.
3. **"Who did this?"** Put your friend into a fast stunt — a bungee jump, a car chase — and caption it as if you just found the footage.
4. **The group-chat roast.** The friend who is always late, cast as someone running at full speed. Caption: "Him on his way to dinner, 40 minutes late."

## Ideas for couples and family

5. **Movie-night couple.** Swap your partner into a cinematic slow walk and caption it like a film trailer: "Coming this summer."
6. **Parents in a music video.** A dance scene with your dad or mum swapped in works because nobody expects it — ask them first, then post the reaction too.
7. **Throwback glow-up.** Use a photo of yourself from ten years ago in a modern, high-energy scene: "Me in 2016 if I'd had confidence."

## Ideas that ride an existing trend

8. **Join the trend that is already moving.** When a clip is everywhere — like the [Crazy Frog chase](/crazy-frog-ai), the [Ozil chip goal](/ozil-ai) or the [Flying Car](/flying-car-ai) stunt — your version gets discovered through the same sound and hashtag.
9. **Use the original sound.** Post with the trending audio in the app; it is what links your clip to everyone else's.
10. **Stitch or duet the original.** Show the original first, then your swapped version — the comparison is the payoff.

## Ideas for creators and small brands

11. **The mascot cameo.** Put your shop owner, coach or team mascot into a trending scene as a lighthearted announcement: "New opening hours, same energy."
12. **Before/after of your own clip.** Upload one of your own steady shots and swap in a team member or a character. Show the original for one second, then the swap.

## How to make a trend post perform

- **Hook in the first second.** Start on the most dramatic frame, not on a slow intro.
- **Post vertical.** 9:16 fills the screen on TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
- **Keep it under 10 seconds** so it loops — loops count as rewatches.
- **Label it as AI** where the platform asks you to; viewers are more forgiving of an obvious edit than a hidden one.
- **Only swap people who said yes.** A friend who agreed to the joke will share it; one who did not will ask you to take it down.
