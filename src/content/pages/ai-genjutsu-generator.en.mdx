This reference explains what the [Genjutsu AI editor](/create) accepts and what its current workflow produces. The generator takes an existing scene and a character photo, then creates a new interpretation of that performance. Browse examples on the [homepage](/), or use the [step-by-step creation tutorial](/guides/how-to-use-genjutsu-ai) for a guided walkthrough.

## Supported inputs and output

| Item            | Supported in this editor                                      | What to check                                           |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Character photo | JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB                                 | One person with a clearly visible face                  |
| Reference video | A built-in effect, or an uploaded MP4 or MOV                  | Choose one clear main performer and a readable action   |
| Clip length     | At least 3 seconds, with up to 10 seconds used for generation | Longer uploads are trimmed to their middle 10 seconds   |
| Framing         | 16:9 or 9:16 for your own reference                           | Built-in effects use the framing shown in their preview |
| Download        | MP4                                                           | Review the completed clip before posting                |

A character photo supplies appearance. The reference video supplies movement, camera work and the scene. The editor needs both kinds of input; choosing a built-in effect provides the video reference for you. The [AI kissing page](/pollo-ai-kissing) has its own two-photo inputs for that scene.

## Choosing an effect or your own clip

The [effect library](/genjutsu-video-ai) describes available scenes and their formats. For a named trend, open [Dime Dime Baby AI](/dime-dime-baby-ai), [Ozil AI](/ozil-ai) or [Crazy Frog AI](/crazy-frog-ai). Each page explains its reference scene and provides the matching creator.

Your own footage gives you a different performance to work from. Check the middle of a long clip before uploading, because that is the portion used when it exceeds 10 seconds. Rapid cuts, a tiny lead or a face hidden throughout the shot can make the result harder to evaluate.

Use a sharp, clear photo that you have permission to edit. A distant group shot or a face covered by sunglasses gives the generator less facial detail. The [photo and scene selection tutorial](/guides/how-to-use-genjutsu-ai) explains these preparation choices in more detail.

## What the generator changes

The current editor recasts the lead from the character photo while using the reference performance to guide the result. It does not guarantee an exact likeness or pixel-identical background. Check faces, hands, motion and surrounding objects in the finished video.

This is an independent service, separate from Higgsfield's official Genjutsu product. For the differences between their editing controls and this site's workflow, use the [Higgsfield Genjutsu alternative comparison](/higgsfield-genjutsu-alternative).

## Does it accept a text-only prompt?

The current creation page is a reference-video character editor. It does not offer a text-only creation form, so you do not need a Seedance prompt for this workflow. If you have no source footage, start with a built-in effect. A text-to-video model's available settings do not describe the controls in this reference editor.

## Price, checkout and failed renders

The first video costs $9.90, paid once with no subscription. Each Genjutsu generation uses $9.90 of prepaid balance for a clip up to 10 seconds. Larger [video packs](/pricing) can reduce the purchase price per video. Browsing examples or creating an account does not include a free render.

The editor saves your photo and reference before sending you to checkout so it can resume when you return. Keep the page open while it prepares the files and starts the render. An interrupted upload can be retried; if the browser can no longer read a selected file, select that photo or video again.

A technical generation failure returns the deducted balance. A successfully rendered video is not automatically refunded because its creative result differs from your expectations. Read the [free-account and rendering-cost guide](/genjutsu-free) for the billing policy before purchasing.
