Every pack is a one-time purchase, with no subscription and no unlimited plan. A swap costs the same $9.90 of balance whether the clip is 3 or 10 seconds long, and the exact amount is shown beside Generate before you submit. The [pricing page](/pricing) always has the current packs.

## What you can do without paying

You can register, browse the available reference templates and watch their examples before buying anything. Use that time to understand the available workflow and decide whether your source video and character photo are suitable. Opening an example does not mean a paid generation has started.

## What requires credits

Submitting a render uses credits because it sends a generation job to a paid model provider. On the current creation page every character swap, from a 3-second clip up to 10 seconds, costs the same $9.90 of balance; the amount is shown beside Generate before you submit.

The minimum one-time purchase is **$9.90**. The [pricing page](/pricing) offers shared balance packs at **$9.90 ($9.90 balance, one 10-second video), $24.90 ($29.70 balance, three 10-second videos) and $39.90 ($49.50 balance, five 10-second videos)**. The balance is shared by every video mode the site offers; the creation page currently offers character swaps. These are paid packs, not free trials or subscriptions. Each swap costs $9.90 of balance, so the larger packs work out at $8.30 and $7.98 per video. That cost is deducted from a purchased balance, not a separate checkout.

## No subscription or automatic renewal

These packs are one-time purchases. Buying a pack does not start a recurring subscription or automatically buy another pack when your balance runs out. Check the current pricing page and checkout for the pack allowance and terms before paying.

## What happens if generation fails?

Credits deducted for a failed generation are automatically returned to your balance. This is a credit refund for a failed task, not a promise of a cash refund for every purchase. A completed video that you do not like creatively is different from a failed generation; review the [refund terms](/terms-of-service) before buying.
