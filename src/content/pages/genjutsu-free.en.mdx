Yes for creating an account, browsing the template library and opening examples; no for rendering your own video. A free account is a way to explore the tool, not a promise of free unlimited generation.

## What you can do without paying

You can register, browse the available reference templates and watch their examples before buying anything. Use that time to understand the available workflow and decide whether your source video and character photo are suitable. Opening an example does not mean a paid generation has started.

## What requires credits

Submitting a render uses credits because it sends a generation job to a paid model provider. The cost depends on the workflow and selected settings. Longer clips, higher resolutions and different models can have different costs, so review the amount beside Generate before submitting.

The smallest one-time purchase is the **Trial Pack at $6.90**, as listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). It is a paid starter pack, not a free trial or a subscription. Pack counts are generation allowances; they are not a promise that every duration and model produces the same number of videos. In the current reference editor, a 5-second clip uses one generation allowance and a 10-second clip uses two.

## No subscription or automatic renewal

These packs are one-time purchases. Buying a pack does not start a recurring subscription or automatically buy another pack when your balance runs out. Check the current pricing page and checkout for the pack allowance and terms before paying.

## What happens if generation fails?

Credits deducted for a failed generation are automatically returned to your balance. This is a credit refund for a failed task, not a promise of a cash refund for every purchase. A completed video that you do not like creatively is different from a failed generation; review the [refund terms](/terms-of-service) before buying.

## Is there a Genjutsu free trial?

There is no free unlimited rendering plan advertised here. “Genjutsu free trial” searches can also refer to Higgsfield's separate service. An offer on another platform does not grant credits on this site, and our Trial Pack is not a Higgsfield trial.

This site is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Higgsfield. Read the [Higgsfield Genjutsu alternative comparison](/higgsfield-genjutsu-alternative) if you arrived expecting that product.
