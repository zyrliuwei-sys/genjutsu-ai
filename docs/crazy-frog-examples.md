# Crazy Frog reference videos

Updated 2026-10-18 at the user's request to use examples from https://crazy-frog-ai.com/ and https://crazyfrogai.org/.

The page contains six locally hosted reference clips (one in the hero and five in the gallery), not outputs of this site's generator or built-in generation templates. Original source URLs are recorded in the manifest. The small bottom-right URL region was repaired using surrounding-edge interpolation at the user’s request, replacing an earlier rectangular blur. Public availability is not represented as a free commercial reuse license.

Six clips are from crazyfrogai.org's public gallery: overpass, neon city, chocolate factory, pixel world, circuit board and Halloween. The hover-bike and red-bike renders from crazy-frog-ai.com were removed at the user’s request. That site's cfo-sample files were excluded because their scenes repeat the other site's gallery.

The original URLs and source/output SHA-256 values are in `crazy-frog-example-sources.json`. All six source hashes differ. Three frames per clip (20%, 50%, 80%) were compared at 32×32 grayscale resolution; no pair was near-identical. A contact sheet was visually checked to confirm distinct characters, locations or chase footage.

Clips are encoded as H.264/AAC MP4 with fast-start metadata. The URL region is repaired in both video and poster; the rest of the framing and original audio are unchanged. JPEG posters are extracted from the corresponding local videos. Players use `preload="none"` and do not autoplay, so the page loads posters instead of downloading six video files up front. No TikTok frames, logos, social rails or platform scripts remain in the gallery.
